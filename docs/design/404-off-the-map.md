## Final generation prompt

Create a production-ready transparent-background illustration asset for the Ombuddi 'Off the map' 404 page. The attached image is the exact character identity reference. Preserve this monster's round purple fluffy body, two upward curved purple/pink striped horns, small tuft, black oval eyes with white highlights, dark outline, cheerful open smile with coral tongue, pink belly, stubby feet, subtle flat paper texture. Full body front view, centered, tightly framed with modest breathing room and complete horns and feet visible. Repose character: one paw holds a large unfolded cream paper map across its belly, the other paw scratches the side of its head in a friendly puzzled way. Raise one eyebrow, keep approachable and gently funny. The map has a simple dark teal dotted wandering route and a small dark teal home icon, no actual geography or text. One small floating lavender question mark beside upper-right horn. Flat hand-drawn textured illustration matching reference, not 3D. Map should not hide face or feet. Very subtle soft grounding shadow only. No UI, no buttons, no wordmark, no 404 numerals, no letters or captions besides the question mark. This image must have genuine alpha transparency around the entire character, map and question mark; do not paint a background or checkerboard.
